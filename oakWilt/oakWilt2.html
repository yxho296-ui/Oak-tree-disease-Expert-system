OAK WILT DETECTION EXPERT SYSTEM

Welcome to Oak Wilt Expert System. The Oak Wilt Detection Expert System is an advanced diagnostic tool designed to identify and manage oak wilt, a deadly disease affecting oak trees. The system leverages artificial intelligence and expert knowledge to provide accurate and timely detection, aiding in the preservation of oak ecosystems and mitigating economic and ecological impacts.

This system will help to detect the presence of oak wilt in three types of oak trees, namely red oak, white oak and live oak. Treatments will also be provided upon oak wilt detected. Click the button "Start" to begin.
